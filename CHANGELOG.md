# Changelog

## 0.8.0 (September 28, 2026)

Download: `runling_0.8.0.apk` from the [Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OKRJil2Vq85TlMJgisvNVxiQkPWyds1h?usp=sharing)
· SHA-256 `1ce0fd1c56b1f5e7c63ff1f676f2eeff78ffde29720c614a4c8d82fadf7a536e`

**New: the Daily Event**
- Once a day, register on the Races tab and race **everyone who signs up**, with AI runners
  filling the empty places (12 per race).
- Registration closes at **7:00 PM Philippine time**; the race runs right after, and your results
  wait on the Races tab with **Watch the race**.
- Bigger prizes (3x a normal race), gear for the podium, and **gold, silver and bronze medals**
  for the top 3.
- Your runner races as they are when you register. Trained or bought gear since? Tap
  **Update my entry** (free) before 7:00 PM.

**New: medals and race bibs**
- Event medals hang on the **medal stand** in your bedroom. Tap it to see every medal: the
  event, your place, time and date.
- **Race bibs:** one per distance, with your races, 1st/2nd/3rd finishes, personal best and
  average pace. Also on the Races tab (**Medals & race bibs**).
- Race results now tell you when you set a **new personal best**.

**Your home**
- Your runner now lives in the house: walks around, opens the fridge, cooks, sits on the bed,
  looks out of the window, stretches.
- **Furniture perks:** every piece does something small, like +3 max energy (sofa), cheaper
  training (rug) or extra race XP (poster, TV). The shop shows each perk.
- The room looks right on tall phones (the window was up near the roof), and the Medal Rack
  moved to the living room.

**Also new**
- **Music and sound effects**, with on/off switches in Menu.
- **Frame rate** setting in Menu: 60 fps, or 30 fps to save battery.
- The log-in screen is centred.

**Updating from 0.7.0:** open the new APK and tap **Update**. You stay logged in and keep
everything.

**First test version for friends.** Welcome to Runling!

- **Your runner:** create a runner that looks like you, with faces, skin
  tones and 10 hairstyles.
- **Real steps:** connect Health Connect and your daily steps power your
  runner's Fitness, energy, coins and XP.
- **Care, training and jobs:** feed your runner, train five stats, and earn
  coins from part-time jobs.
- **Races:** five race tiers from the free Community Fun Run to the
  Marathon, in changing conditions (road, track, trail, rain, mud), against
  AI runners matched to your league.
- **Gear, home and quests:** a gear shop with outfit sets and dyes, a room to
  furnish (and a living room from level 15), daily and weekly quests, a
  monthly walking milestone and a login calendar.
- **New in this version:**
  - A "Getting started" guide for new players: feed your runner, run your
    first race and train a stat.
  - Prestige rewards: the Champion Singlet and title at level 40, and the
    Laurel Crown and Legend title at level 50.
  - Accounts: confirm your email with a code, reset a forgotten password
    with a code, and delete your account from the Menu.
