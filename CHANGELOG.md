## 0.10.0 (September 29, 2026)

Download: `runling_0.10.0.apk` from the [Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OKRJil2Vq85TlMJgisvNVxiQkPWyds1h?usp=sharing)
· SHA-256 `43e8333d0c9e76fad1eafae1b4e784baf0cd982069259f077e5ba2ff7b66bd75`

Installs over 0.9.0; your runner and progress stay. **Please update:** 0.9.0 can't draw the new
hairstyles and faces.

**New: boss challenges**
- Five boss runners wait at the bottom of the Races tab, one for each distance: Pepper
  Quickstep (from level 3), Rio Dashwell (6), Mika Stormrun (12), Kaito Ironlung (22) and
  Solenne Voss (32). Each races one on one, on their own home ground.
- A try costs the race's energy. Beat a boss once for a big coin and XP reward and a
  **Challenge medal**; after that, rematches are free (just for fun, no rewards).
- Bosses never get stronger, you do: train, walk more to raise your Fitness, eat well and gear up.
- Challenge medals have their own purple-and-gold look and their own tab in the medal list.

**New: quest bonuses**
- Finish **all** your daily quests for an extra +20¢ and +30 XP, and all your weekly quests for
  +100¢ and +150 XP.
- **Perfect Month:** claim the daily bonus on 20 days of a month (missed days are fine) for
  200 XP and, the first time, the exclusive **Perfect Month Cap**.
- Each daily quest now pays 25¢ (was 30¢); finishing them all pays more than before.

**New: more ways to look like you**
- Hairstyles now match the body type, with a few for both. Seven new ones: side part, mohawk,
  slicked back and messy; pigtails, braid and long wavy. Your current hairstyle stays.
- Four new eye styles (sparkle, almond, lashes, wink), three new noses, and new choices for
  **eyebrows** and **mouths**.

**New: unique names**
- Every runner's name is now one of a kind. If a name is taken, the game suggests a free one.
- If someone else had your name first, the game asks you for a new one (you can do it later
  from **Menu → Change runner name**).

**New: feature tips**
- When a level unlocks something new, a short card explains it, with a **Show me** button.

## 0.9.0 (September 28, 2026)

Download: `runling_0.9.0.apk` from the [Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OKRJil2Vq85TlMJgisvNVxiQkPWyds1h?usp=sharing)

Installs over 0.8.x; your runner and progress stay. **Please update:** the races changed on the
server, so older versions show the wrong conditions.

**New: weather**
- Every race now rolls its own weather when it starts, the same for everyone in it:
  - **Rain** (1 in 4 races): slippery and a bit unpredictable. Luck (speed bursts) and
    Strength (grip) help.
  - **Heat** (about 1 in 7): runners tire sooner. Stamina helps.
  - Clear the rest of the time.
- The ground (track, road, trail, gravel, mud) still changes once a day and is shown on the
  Races tab. Weather comes on top, so rain on a muddy trail is the toughest race there is.
- The race title and scenery show the weather: dark skies and rain, or a hot orange sky.

**New: named Events**
- Every day's Event has its own name, like **Harbor Lights 10K** or **Pine Ridge Marathon**,
  and the same name never comes back for 100 days.
- Podium medals carry the name and the date, so each one is a one-of-a-kind keepsake.
- After registration closes, the Races tab shows tomorrow's Event.

## 0.8.0 (September 28, 2026)

Download: `runling_0.8.0.apk` from the [Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OKRJil2Vq85TlMJgisvNVxiQkPWyds1h?usp=sharing)
· SHA-256 `1ce0fd1c56b1f5e7c63ff1f676f2eeff78ffde29720c614a4c8d82fadf7a536e`

**New: the Daily Event**
- Once a day, register on the Races tab and race **everyone who signs up**, with AI runners
  filling the empty places (12 per race).
- Registration closes at **7:00 PM Philippine time**; the race runs right after, and your results
  wait on the Races tab with **Watch the race**.
- Bigger prizes (3x a normal race), gear for the podium, and **gold, silver and bronze medals**
  for the top 3.
- Your runner races as they are when you register. Trained or bought gear since? Tap
  **Update my entry** (free) before 7:00 PM.

**New: medals and race bibs**
- Event medals hang on the **medal stand** in your bedroom. Tap it to see every medal: the
  event, your place, time and date.
- **Race bibs:** one per distance, with your races, 1st/2nd/3rd finishes, personal best and
  average pace. Also on the Races tab (**Medals & race bibs**).
- Race results now tell you when you set a **new personal best**.

**Your home**
- Your runner now lives in the house: walks around, opens the fridge, cooks, sits on the bed,
  looks out of the window, stretches.
- **Furniture perks:** every piece does something small, like +3 max energy (sofa), cheaper
  training (rug) or extra race XP (poster, TV). The shop shows each perk.
- The room looks right on tall phones (the window was up near the roof), and the Medal Rack
  moved to the living room.

**Also new**
- **Music and sound effects**, with on/off switches in Menu.
- **Frame rate** setting in Menu: 60 fps, or 30 fps to save battery.
- The log-in screen is centred.

**Updating from 0.7.0:** open the new APK and tap **Update**. You stay logged in and keep
everything.

**First test version for friends.** Welcome to Runling!

- **Your runner:** create a runner that looks like you, with faces, skin
  tones and 10 hairstyles.
- **Real steps:** connect Health Connect and your daily steps power your
  runner's Fitness, energy, coins and XP.
- **Care, training and jobs:** feed your runner, train five stats, and earn
  coins from part-time jobs.
- **Races:** five race tiers from the free Community Fun Run to the
  Marathon, in changing conditions (road, track, trail, rain, mud), against
  AI runners matched to your league.
- **Gear, home and quests:** a gear shop with outfit sets and dyes, a room to
  furnish (and a living room from level 15), daily and weekly quests, a
  monthly walking milestone and a login calendar.
- **New in this version:**
  - A "Getting started" guide for new players: feed your runner, run your
    first race and train a stat.
  - Prestige rewards: the Champion Singlet and title at level 40, and the
    Laurel Crown and Legend title at level 50.
  - Accounts: confirm your email with a code, reset a forgotten password
    with a code, and delete your account from the Menu.
